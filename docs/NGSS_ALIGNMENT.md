# NGSS alignment

Performance-expectation text paraphrases NGSS Lead States (2013). Check the official NGSS site and your district framework for exact wording and evidence statements.

| Standard | Practice / core idea | What students do in this app | Evidence produced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HS-LS2-1**: use mathematical and/or computational representations to support explanations of factors that affect carrying capacity | Computational thinking; LS2.A | Investigation 1: vary nutrient loading and light, and read the limiting-factor values f_light, f_N, f_P. Investigation 2: increase stocking until the tank can no longer support the fish, and identify the oxygen and ammonia limits. Compare aeration settings. | Recorded-results table; fish biomass and algae charts; CER write-up |
| **HS-LS2-4**: use mathematical representations to support claims for the cycling of matter and flow of energy | Math & computational thinking; LS2.B | Investigation 4: compute growth efficiency (kJ stored ÷ kJ eaten) and the share of algal energy reaching fish; compare with the ~10 % rule. Investigation 5: use grams of N moved by each process and the mass-balance table (change in storage = inputs − exports). | Calculations in the lab report; nitrogen diagram values |
| **HS-LS2-5**: develop a model of photosynthesis and cellular respiration in carbon cycling | Developing & using models; LS2.B, PS3.D | Investigation 5: trace carbon through CO₂ → algae → fish/decomposers → CO₂, with exchange with the air, using the carbon diagram. Day/night oxygen band (photosynthesis vs respiration). Students identify carbon sources and sinks. | Carbon diagram reading; explanation of the day/night O₂ swing |
| **HS-LS2-7**: design, evaluate and refine a solution for reducing the impacts of human activities on the environment and biodiversity | Designing solutions; LS2.C, LS4.D, ETS1.B | Investigation 3: design a Waste-to-Plate system (crop density, harvest interval, aeration, feeding); evaluate nutrients, algae, fish survival and food produced; refine. Investigation 6: relate the design to nutrient pollution measured in a real watershed (Saginaw River). | Design comparison table; trade-off reflection; real-data analysis |
